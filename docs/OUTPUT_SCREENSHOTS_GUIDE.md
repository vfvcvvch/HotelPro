# HotelPro - Output Screenshots Guide

For your **SkillWallet** portal submission under **Epic 6: Project Execution (Task 18: Output Screenshots)**, here are the key screens to capture:

---

### 📸 Recommended Screenshots for Evaluation:

1. **Homepage & MakeMyTrip Hero Search**
   * *URL:* `http://localhost:5173/`
   * *What to show:* The Navy gradient hero banner, destination dropdown (Goa, Mumbai, Jaipur), date selectors, and "MMT Assured" badges.

2. **Room Catalog & Interactive Filter Sidebar**
   * *URL:* `http://localhost:5173/rooms?city=Goa`
   * *What to show:* Left filter sidebar with active price slider, category radio buttons, and the list of room cards with photo previews and price tags.

3. **Room Details & Photo Lightbox**
   * *URL:* `http://localhost:5173/rooms/r201`
   * *What to show:* Multi-photo gallery grid, room specs (sq.ft, bed type, guests), amenities checklist, verified reviews, and sticky booking card.

4. **Reservation Checkout & Discount Modal**
   * *Trigger:* Click "BOOK NOW" on any room card
   * *What to show:* Guest details form, check-in/out dates, and the price breakdown with promo code **`HOTELPRO20`** (20% discount).

5. **Simulated Payment Gateway**
   * *Trigger:* Click "PROCEED TO PAYMENT" in checkout modal
   * *What to show:* Card, UPI QR, and NetBanking tabs with secure 256-bit SSL encrypted badge.

6. **Official Tax Invoice & Stay Voucher**
   * *Trigger:* Automatically appears after payment success (or from "View Tax Invoice" button)
   * *What to show:* Clean corporate invoice layout, Booking ID, GST tax breakdown, QR code, and the "Print / Save PDF" button.

7. **Guest Dashboard (`/my-bookings`)**
   * *URL:* `http://localhost:5173/my-bookings`
   * *What to show:* Reservation cards with `CONFIRMED` or `CHECKED-IN` status chips, and the 1-click "Cancel Stay" button.

8. **Hotel Staff Operations Portal (`/staff`)**
   * *URL:* `http://localhost:5173/staff`
   * *What to show:* Today's Arrivals queue with "CHECK-IN GUEST" button and the Housekeeping Room Matrix grid (`Clean`, `Dirty`, `Occupied`, `Maintenance`).

9. **Admin Executive Analytics & Console (`/admin`)**
   * *URL:* `http://localhost:5173/admin`
   * *What to show:* Total Revenue turnover card (₹), Monthly Revenue Bar Chart, Room Occupancy Distribution, and the Dynamic Pricing editor.

---

### 💡 Tip for High Marks:
Open the browser at full screen (`1920x1080` or `1366x768`) and use `Win + Shift + S` on Windows to take crisp, clean snips of each view.
